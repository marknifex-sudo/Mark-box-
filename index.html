Stream and download the movies and shows you own. Mark Box signs in to your Jellyfin server; your login is saved only on this device.

How to set up Jellyfin (free, about 15 minutes)
  1. On your computer, install Jellyfin Server, create a user, and add a library pointing at your movies and shows folder.
  2. Install Tailscale on that computer and on your phone, signed in to the same account.
  3. On the computer, open a terminal and run tailscale serve --bg 8096. It prints an address like https://my-pc.tailnet-name.ts.net. Only your own devices can open it.
  4. Paste that address above with your Jellyfin username and password, then tap Connect library.

Mark Box is served over https, so the browser only allows an https server address. A plain http://192.168… address will be blocked. If your browser asks to allow access to devices on your network, choose Allow.
